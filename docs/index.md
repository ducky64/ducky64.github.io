## About

Quack!

I build [circuit boards](boards.md) for fun, and build the [open-source Python-based HDL](https://github.com/BerkeleyHCI/PolymorphicBlocks) for building those boards.

Previously, I was an [academic researcher](research.md) at the odd intersection of programming languages, electronics design, and human-computer interaction. I completed my PhD at UC Berkeley, co-advised by Björn Hartmann and Elad Alon, and then did a postdoc at UCLA, as part of Ankur Mehta's LEMUR lab.
